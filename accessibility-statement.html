Accessibility Statement

This statement was last updated in October 2026.

We at A. M. French are working to make our site, amfrench.com, accessible to people with disabilities.

What Web Accessibility Is

An accessible site allows visitors with disabilities to browse the site with the same or a similar level of ease and enjoyment as other visitors. This can be achieved with the capabilities of the system on which the site is operating, and through assistive technologies.

Accessibility Adjustments on This Site

We aim to follow the WCAG 2.1 guidelines at level AA. As part of this effort, we have:

  • Set the language of the site
  • Set a logical content order on each page
  • Defined clear heading structures on all pages
  • Added alternative text to images
  • Made the site usable with a keyboard, including a “Skip to main content” link
  • Reduced motion for visitors who have asked their device to minimize animation
