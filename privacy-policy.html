Privacy Policy

Thank you for visiting Hunkie Dorie!

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you visit our website or make a purchase.

1. Information We Collect

We collect certain information from you when you:

  • Place an order in our shop
  • Subscribe to our newsletter
  • Fill out a contact form
  • Browse our website

The information may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Shipping and billing address
  • Payment information (processed securely by our payment provider – we never store full credit card details)
  • Browser type, IP address, and other analytics data (collected automatically to help us improve our website)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Send order confirmations and updates
  • Respond to your messages or questions
  • Send occasional newsletters or special updates (only if you opt in)
  • Improve our website, products, and user experience

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Sharing Your Information

We only share your information with trusted third parties necessary to run our business, such as:

  • Payment processors (e.g., Stripe, PayPal)
  • Shipping carriers
  • Email newsletter providers (e.g., Mailchimp)

These partners are only authorized to use your information as needed to provide their services.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, analyze site traffic, and remember your preferences. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies if you prefer.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information
  • Withdraw consent for communications at any time
  • Request a copy of your data

To exercise these rights, please contact us at contact@amfrench.com.

6. Children’s Privacy

We value the privacy of young readers!

Our website and shop are directed to adults (parents, teachers, and gift buyers), not to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we’ve collected such data in error, please contact us and we’ll delete it promptly.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from loss, misuse, or unauthorized access using secure servers and encrypted connections.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at contact@amfrench.com.
